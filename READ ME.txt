This project is a simple messaging app that i want to make since alot of the current messaging apps are, to me, bad in their own ways.
Dont expect much as i am new to python, and this is more or less to show not only what im capable of, but as a showcase to any employer who is looking into my work
when deciding to hire me. 

==================================================================

Scope: This app will be akin to discord, a simple app that will allow text based messaging between 2 people, or a group of people. I tend to grow this into a
platform of its own, but for now this is only going to be an open source messaging app for personal use until it is ready to become something bigger.

Disclaimer: This is a solo project. there is no team nor anything else helping me write this code so updates will be slow and infrequent. I am currently learning
python at freecodecamp.com a website that is showing me the fundamentals of python, which is allowing me to make this program from scratch. i am also learning everything
as i go, so if you see something within this code that you believe there is a better way of doing, you are most likely correct, and i either didn't know that way existed,
or didn't think of it at the time. 

Stage 1 goal: send messages back and forth between two devices.
Stage 2 goal: send images back and forth between two devices.
stage 3 goal: send messages/images into a group chat.
stage 4 goal: voice chatting between two devices.
stage 5 goal: video chatting between two devices.

More will be added but these are the main goals i have in mind. i will be expanding this list or rewriting it once these initial 5 goals are completed.
